Project 01 • Business Analysis Case Study

Reimagining Sociolla's Click & Collect Experience

From identifying operational challenges to designing a seamless omnichannel pickup experience.

Business Problem

Click & Collect relied on manual store confirmation via chat (WhatsApp), taking 81–178 mins. 51% of users did not complete payment.

Proposed Solution

Auto-Confirmation Feature: Automated system validation for payment & stock, updating order status instantly.

Deliverables

PRD, Competitor Benchmark, User Stories, AS-IS vs TO-BE BPMN, User Flow, High-Fidelity Prototype (Figma).

Key Research & Quantitative Findings:

  • Average failed confirmation time: 178 minutes | Successful: 81 minutes
  • 51% of users did not complete payment due to delays.
  • Average order value at risk: Rp650,000 - Rp940,000
  • Design principles applied: Clarity, Trust, and Convenience
Project 02 • Business Intelligence Dashboard

Tracing the Sales Loss Report (Amazon India Q2 2022)

From uncovering revenue loss patterns to delivering data-driven business insights.

Objective & Tools

Identify revenue loss drivers using SQL, Excel, and Power BI with a Star Schema Data Model.

Key Impact Identified

8.80% Lost Revenue (6.92M INR) due to cancellations across transactions.

Critical Triangle

Maharashtra (13.3M INR), Karnataka (10.5M INR), and Telangana (6.9M INR) identified as growth regions.

Strategic Pillars & Insights:

  • Revenue Growth & Efficiency: Monitored AOV and proved promotions drive high-value carts.
  • Financial Leakage Mitigation: Converted cancellation rates into monetary impact (6.92M INR).
  • Operational Optimization: Recommended local courier audits in top cancelled regions.
