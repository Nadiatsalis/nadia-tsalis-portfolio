Business Analysis Case Study Omnichannel Retail Project 01

Reimagining Sociolla's Click & Collect Experience

Analyzing operational bottlenecks in Sociolla's Click & Collect service and designing an automated order confirmation solution to reduce delay, minimize drop-off rates, and improve customer satisfaction.

Project Overview

Sociolla is a leading beauty omnichannel platform in Indonesia that integrates online and offline shopping experiences. This project focuses on optimizing the Click & Collect feature (order online, pick up in-store) by eliminating slow manual verifications.

Role

Business Analyst / Requirement Analyst

Process modeling, user research, feature prioritization & PRD.

Core Objective

Automate Order Validation

Transition from manual WhatsApp chat checks to instant system checks.

Key Impact Goal

Reduce Confirmation Time

Targeting faster pickup readiness and capturing at-risk revenue.

Business Problem & Research Findings

Click & Collect orders previously relied on manual confirmation: store staff had to check physical stock and manually confirm availability via chat before customers could collect orders. This created long wait times and high drop-off rates.

Quantitative Insights

  • Analyzed transaction data comparing successful vs. failed confirmations.
  • Identified that most order drop-offs occur during High Store Busy Levels.
  • Manual validation failed to scale during peak shopping hours.

Qualitative Insights (User Interviews)

  • Interviewed representative users (Khansa, Dillah, Mike).
  • Users reported that waiting for confirmation chat felt frustratingly slow.
  • Inconsistent stock information led to unexpected order cancellations.
  • Strong user demand for real-time order status tracking.

Business Process Modeling (BPMN)

Redesigning the end-to-end Click & Collect workflow from a manual, multi-step communication chain to an automated real-time process.

AS-IS Manual & Time-Consuming Process
1. Order Placed Customer places Click & Collect order.
2. HQ Admin Contact HQ Admin contacts Store Leader via chat.
3. Manual Stock Check Store staff physically checks inventory.
4. Store Replies Chat Confirms stock availability back to HQ.
5. Customer Waits Long delay before collection confirmation.

Bottleneck: Manual steps cause severe delays, communication errors, and fail during high foot-traffic hours.

TO-BE Automated & Real-Time Solution
1. Order Placed Customer submits Click & Collect checkout.
2. Auto Validation System automatically validates payment & stock.
3. Auto Confirmation Order status updates instantly in system.
4. Store Notified Store receives immediate prep notification.
5. Real-Time Update Customer gets pickup estimate & QR code.

Solution Impact: Instant confirmation, zero reliance on manual chat, and higher order completion.
