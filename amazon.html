Tracing Sales Loss Report — Amazon India Q2 2022

Uncovering potential revenue leakage in Amazon India transactions by analyzing cancellation patterns, promotion effectiveness, regional performance gaps, and shipping methods using SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

Project Overview

During Q2 2022 (April – June 2022), Amazon India experienced high transaction volumes but suffered from noticeable revenue leakage. This project performs an end-to-end data analysis to convert raw transaction records into strategic revenue recovery initiatives.

The Financial Leakage

A total of 6.92 Million INR failed to materialize in Q2 2022 due to order cancellations. Converting the 8.80% cancellation rate into tangible monetary value established immediate urgency to audit logistics and payment gateways.

Logistics Dependency Risk

70% of total orders rely directly on Amazon's internal fulfillment network. While maintaining service consistency, this over-reliance creates significant margin risks if operational costs rise or local courier bottlenecks occur.

3 Strategic Pillars for Revenue Recovery

Translating data exploration into 3 key analytical pillars to drive operational expansion and financial health.

1. Revenue Growth & Efficiency

AOV & Promo Analysis:

Compared Average Order Value (AOV) between promo and non-promo users. Proved that promotions effectively drive higher cart values rather than just discount seekers.

2. Leakage Mitigation

Top Cancelled Regions:

Identified the 3 provinces with the highest cancellation volumes as an early warning trigger for local operational and fulfillment investigations.

3. Operational & Logistics

Fulfillment Optimization:

Mapped carrier shipping methods against failure rates to provide concrete evidence for logistics vendor evaluation and SLA enforcement.

The "Critical Triangle" Priority Regions

Market demand in these three regions is exceptionally high, making them the most impactful targets for immediate cancellation reduction and promo expansion.

Power BI Dashboard & Actionable Recommendations

Interactive visual dashboard built in Power BI to give executive stakeholders clear visibility over sales performance and regional risks.

Final Strategic Action Plan

Implement targeted promotional expansions in the Critical Triangle (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana) to boost shopping cart values, while conducting immediate audits of local logistics and stock availability to minimize cancellations and reduce absolute reliance on a single fulfillment channel.
